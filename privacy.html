Privacy policy

Policy version 1.0, effective 24 November 2026.

In short

  • Everything you log stays on your phone. We never receive it.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • You can export or delete everything in Settings, any time.

Who we are

Rhythmio is made by an independent developer in the Netherlands. Contact: support.rhythmio.app@gmail.com.

What the app stores

Only on your phone:

  • Cycle and check-ins: period dates, flow, cramps, headache, focus, mood, energy and irritability.
  • Medicine (optional): the time of each intake, when it kicked in, felt strongest and wore off, your "Meds helped" rating and notes. The app never asks for the medicine's name or how much you take.
  • Settings and progress: setup answers, reminder time, tips you tried, your "Plan for it" checklist, your consents, and the ids of Rhythmio's calendar events if you use Calendar sync.

The app stores health data only with your explicit consent (GDPR Article 9(2)(a)) and uses it only to run its features for you (Article 6(1)(b)). It's kept in an encrypted database whose key is in the iOS Keychain, and it's left out of iCloud and computer backups, so it doesn't move to a new phone unless you export it first. Reminders are created on the phone, and their text never names a symptom or a medicine. Anyone who can unlock your phone can open Rhythmio, so use a passcode or Face ID.

What leaves your phone

Only what you choose:

  • Export: a file with all your data, to save or share as you like.
  • Calendar sync: your next 3 predicted periods, in a separate "Rhythmio" calendar in your default calendar account (such as iCloud or Google), with discreet names unless you choose clear ones. That service syncs them under its own policy. The app doesn't read your other calendars, and turning sync off removes the Rhythmio calendar.

What we may receive

  • Purchases: Apple handles payment and sends us sales reports that don't name you.
  • Crash reports: only if you turn on "Share with App Developers" in iOS settings. They contain nothing you logged.
  • Beta feedback (TestFlight): what you send, with screenshots, device model and iOS version, and your name and email if you were invited by email. Please leave your health details out.
  • Emails you send us.

We use these only to fix problems, improve the app and answer you (legitimate interest, GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). We keep emails up to 12 months after answering, and beta feedback until the beta ends.

What we never do

  • Sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for ads, research or training AI.
  • Use analytics, tracking or third-party code that sends data anywhere.
  • Ask for your name, email, date of birth or gender. Setup only asks you to confirm you're 16 or older.

Who else is involved

Apple (purchases, crash reports, TestFlight) and GitHub, which hosts our website. Each works under its own privacy policy, and Apple may process its data outside the EU under its own safeguards. The website has no cookies, analytics or tracking; GitHub sees visitors' IP addresses to deliver the pages.

We have no copy of your cycle, check-in or medicine data, so there is nothing to hand over to police or authorities. We disclose emails or feedback only when the law requires it.

Your rights

  • In Settings you can view, correct, export and delete your data, and turn medicine tracking or Calendar sync off. Turning medicine off hides it but keeps your past medicine logs until you delete all data.
  • You can withdraw consent in Settings. The app can't work without the health-data consent, so withdrawing it means deleting your data; the app asks first.
  • For emails or feedback we hold, write to us to see, correct, export or delete them, or to object. We answer within one month.
  • You can complain to the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl) or the data protection authority in your EU country.

Children

Rhythmio is not for anyone under 16.

Problems and changes

If a security problem ever puts your data at risk, or before an important change to this policy, we'll say so on our website and in the app's update notes, and we'll inform the authorities where the law requires it. If a change needs your consent again, the app will ask before it applies. Write to us for an earlier version of this policy.

Rhythmio is a wellness app, not medical advice.
