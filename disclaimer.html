Policy version 1.0, effective 24 November 2026.

In short: Rhythmio helps you notice patterns. It is not a doctor, not a diagnosis and not birth control.

Not medical advice

Rhythmio is a wellness and self-tracking app. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition, including ADHD, PMDD or other menstrual or mental health conditions. Patterns and tips are for your own awareness and to help you talk with a healthcare professional.

Predictions are estimates

Period, ovulation and "harder day" predictions are averages of what you log. Cycles change. Don't use Rhythmio as contraception or to plan or avoid pregnancy.

Medicine

The felt wave is drawn only from how you say your medicine feels. Rhythmio does not estimate medicine levels or give dosing advice. Never start, stop or change your medicine, or when you take it, without talking to your doctor.

Tips

Tips are general ideas. Skip anything that doesn't suit your body, health or situation.

If you need help now

In an emergency, call 112. If you're having thoughts of harming yourself, contact a crisis line: in the Netherlands, call 113 or 0800-0113.
