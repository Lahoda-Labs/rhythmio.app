Where is my data?

On your iPhone only, in an encrypted database. We can't see it, so we can't recover it for you: if you delete the app, the data is gone.

How do I delete everything?

In the app: Today → Settings → Delete all data. You can also export everything first from Settings.

How do I cancel Rhythmio Plus?

In your iPhone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Rhythmio. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews.

Is this medical advice?

No. Rhythmio helps you notice your own patterns. It doesn't diagnose, treat or give medicine advice. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Rhythmio is a wellness app, not medical advice.
